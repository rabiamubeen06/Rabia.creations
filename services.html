How it works

Services & commissions

From a first message to a framed painting on your wall — here's exactly how a commission runs, what it costs, and what people usually ask before they start.

The Process

Four steps, start to finish

Share your idea

Send a reference photo, a Pinterest board, or just a description — plus the size and category you're thinking of.

Get a quote & sketch

You'll receive a price, an estimated delivery date, and a rough sketch to confirm composition before any paint goes down.

Watch it come to life

Progress photos are shared at key stages, so you can flag anything before the final layers and varnish are applied.

Delivery & framing

Your piece is framed, wrapped securely, and shipped or hand-delivered locally, ready to hang straight out of the box.
