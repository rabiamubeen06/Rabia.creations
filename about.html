Meet the artist

The hands behind every canvas

Rabia's Creations is a one-woman studio — every piece you see in the gallery was sketched, mixed and painted by Rabia herself.

Since a school sketchbook

Painting started as a way to slow down

Rabia picked up a brush long before she picked up a client — painting was the thing she did between exams to quiet a busy mind. Friends started asking for copies of what she'd painted for herself, and Rabia's Creations grew out of those first few requests.

Today the studio takes on a small number of commissions at a time, on purpose. Fewer pieces in progress means more attention on each one — from the first pencil sketch to the final coat of varnish.

Every canvas is still mixed and painted from a home studio, by hand, with ordinary acrylics, oils and a lot of reference photos sent in by clients like you.

How We Work

What guides every commission

Nothing is templated

No two commissions use the same sketch. Even a repeated theme, like a name painting, is reworked in fresh colours and composition for that client.

You see it before it's final

You'll get a rough sketch and a progress photo before the final coat, so there's room to adjust colours, sizing or details early.

Small batches, close attention

Only a handful of pieces are in progress at any time — that's what keeps the brushwork detailed instead of rushed.
